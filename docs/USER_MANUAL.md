# Part A — Client manual

## A1. Opening the app for the first time

**What you need:** any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) on a phone or computer, and an internet connection for the first load.

1. Open the Lydia Contact Center web address in your browser.
2. The app decides what to show you based on your screen size:
   - **Phone:** a three-slide welcome carousel.
   - **Tablet or computer:** a two-column welcome page with a photo on the left and text on the right.
3. Nothing is asked of you yet — no account, no email, no phone number.

> You can use the app without installing it. Installing is optional — see [A18](#a18-installing-the-app-on-your-phone-or-computer).

## A2. Mobile intro screens (3 slides)

1. **Slide 1 — "Your safe space for confidential support."** Explains that no personal data is collected.
2. Tap **Next** (bottom button) to continue.
3. **Slide 2 — "Access accurate and expert guidance."** Explains that information comes from trusted sources.
4. Tap **Next** again.
5. **Slide 3 — "Designed for the youth of Ghana."** Tap **Get Started** to finish the intro.
6. On any slide you can tap **Skip** (top-right corner) to jump straight past the intro.
7. The three dots/short bars under the text show which slide you are on: the wide bar is your current slide.

After the last slide you land on the onboarding questions in [A4](#a4-onboarding--naming-your-chatbot).

## A3. Desktop welcome page

1. The left half shows the welcome photo; the right half shows the heading **"Safe Space for Confidential Support"**.
2. The three bullets below the paragraph are: *Fully anonymous*, *Expert guidance*, *Designed for Ghana*.
3. Click **Get Started** to continue to onboarding.
4. There is no login button on this page — clients never sign in.

## A4. Onboarding — naming your chatbot

1. You will see the heading **"Meet Your AI Companion"** and the question **"What would you like to call me?"**
2. Type the name you want the chatbot to use (for example *Ada*, *Kojo*, *Friend*).
3. Tap **Continue** to keep the name, or **Skip (Use default name)** to use the default name **Friend**.
4. The name appears in the sidebar, the chat header and beside every chatbot message. You can change it later in [Settings](#a17-settings).

## A5. Onboarding — age, gender and region

1. Answer the three questions on this screen:
   - **Age range:** 10–14, 15–19, 20–24, 25+
   - **Gender identity:** Female, Male, Non-binary, Prefer not to say
   - **Region in Ghana:** pick your region from the dropdown (all 16 regions are listed)
2. The **Start Chat** button stays disabled until all three answers are chosen.
3. Tap **Start Chat**.
4. Use the back arrow if you need to return to the previous step.
5. These answers are used only to tailor guidance and to produce anonymous statistics. They are tied to an anonymous session, not to your identity.

## A6. The nickname prompt

1. Straight after onboarding, a dialog asks for a nickname so the chatbot can greet you.
2. Type a nickname (up to 20 characters).
3. Choose **Continue** to save it, or **Skip** to stay anonymous.
4. If you skip, you are shown as **Anonymous** everywhere, and the greeting falls back to the anonymous label.
5. You can set or change it later in [Settings → Identity & Profile](#a17-settings).

## A7. Finding your way around

**On a phone**

1. Tap the **☰ menu** icon at the top-left of the header to open the navigation drawer.
2. The drawer lists: **Safe Chat**, **Your Story**, **Myth Buster**, **Support**, **Pharmacy Hub**, **Settings**.
3. Below the list: **Clear Conversation**, **End Session**, and the language buttons.
4. Tap any item to go there; the drawer closes automatically.

**On a tablet or computer**

1. The navigation is permanently visible in the left sidebar (280 px).
2. The sidebar header shows the chatbot avatar, its name and an **Encrypted** indicator.
3. The same sections, privacy actions and languages are listed there.

**The top header (both layouts)** shows:

- The chatbot name and a green **Online** badge.
- Your nickname with a **Welcome back, …** greeting, or **Anonymous**.
- A green **headset** button — *Talk to a live counselor* (see [A12](#a12-talking-to-a-live-counselor)).
- On small screens, the ☰ menu button.

## A8. Safe Chat — sending messages

1. Select **Safe Chat** in the navigation.
2. The first message from the chatbot is already there, with quick-reply buttons underneath such as *Tell me about contraception*, *What are STIs?*, *What are signs of pregnancy?*
3. To use a quick reply, tap it — it is sent exactly like a typed message.
4. To type instead, tap the message box at the bottom and enter your question.
5. Press **Enter** or tap the round blue **send** button.
6. While the answer is generated you see a typing indicator; the answer then types itself out.
7. Under each chatbot answer you may see small details such as **Answered in … ms** and **Language: …**.
8. Follow-up suggestion chips can appear under an answer; tapping one sends it as your next message.
9. If the connection drops mid-answer, the app posts a message asking you to check your internet or tap the headset for a live counselor. Your earlier messages stay on screen.
10. Your conversation is saved on the device for the retention period you choose (see [A17](#a17-settings)).

## A9. Safe Chat — voice input and audio playback

**Speaking instead of typing**

1. Tap the **microphone** icon to the right of the message box.
2. Allow microphone access if the browser asks.
3. Wait for **Listening…** and speak your question.
4. The recognised text is placed in the message box. Review it, then press send.
5. Tap the microphone again to stop listening early.

**Listening to an answer**

1. Under any message, tap the **speaker** icon.
2. The message is read aloud.
3. Tap the **pause** icon on the same message to stop playback.
4. Audio uses the language detected for that answer where supported.

> Voice input and speech playback need a browser that supports them (Chrome, Edge, Safari). If nothing happens, type instead.

## A10. Safe Chat — rating and reporting answers

1. Under each chatbot answer there are three small icons: **thumbs up**, **thumbs down** and **flag**.
2. Tap **thumbs up** if the answer helped — the icon turns green.
3. Tap **thumbs down** if it was unhelpful or wrong — the icon turns red.
4. Tap **flag** to report a problematic answer:
   1. A **Report Message** dialog opens.
   2. Type the reason in the box.
   3. Confirm to submit; the flag icon turns orange and cannot be sent again for that message.
   4. Cancel closes the dialog without reporting.
5. Reports go to the care team for review.

## A11. Safe Chat — when you see "AI assistant paused"

The care team can switch the AI assistant off at any time (for example during a safety incident or service maintenance).

1. When this happens a pale-yellow banner appears above the conversation: **"AI assistant paused"** with the note *Automated replies are temporarily off*.
2. The message box placeholder changes to **"AI paused — tap the headset for a counselor"**.
3. If you send a message, you receive: *"The AI assistant is paused right now. Tap the headset button to reach a live counselor, or call 1221 if this is an emergency."* No message is sent to the AI.
4. Tap the **headset** button, or call **1221**, to reach a human. Live counselor chat keeps working while the AI is off.
5. The banner disappears on its own when the care team restarts the AI.

## A12. Talking to a live counselor

There are three ways to ask for a human; all of them lead to the same request.

**Method 1 — the headset button next to the message box**

1. Tap the round green **headset** icon to the left of the message box.
2. A dialog opens explaining the live counselor option.
3. Confirm to send the request. Your chat is queued for a consultant.

**Method 2 — the header consultant button**

1. Tap the green consultant button in the top header.
2. The **Connect with a Consultant** modal opens (*100% free & confidential*) with two tiles: **Chat** and **Call**.
3. Tap **Chat** to jump to Safe Chat and send the request automatically. If a request is already open, the tile carries an **Active** or **Queued** tag.
4. Tap **Call** to dial the consultant number shown.

**Method 3 — just ask in the chat**

1. Type something like *"I would like to speak to a human"*.
2. The request is recognised and queued automatically.

**What happens next**

1. The header button changes to an amber **queued** state while you wait.
2. When a consultant accepts, the button turns green **live** and a blue **Live Consultant Chat** banner appears at the top of the chat.
3. A counselor's message appears with a **Live Agent** label and a headphones avatar.
4. While a counselor is live, the AI stays silent — you are talking to a person.
5. The message box placeholder changes to **"Message your live counselor…"**.
6. You can keep typing normally; the counselor sees your messages and replies in the same thread.
7. When the counselor releases the chat or ends it, the AI resumes and the banner disappears.

> If no counselor is available you stay queued. In an emergency do not wait — call **1221**, or one of the numbers in [A15](#a15-support).

## A13. Your Story (story mode)

1. Select **Your Story** in the navigation.
2. You see a catalogue of module cards, each with an icon, title and short description. Modules cover puberty and body changes, menstrual health, consent and boundaries, contraception and family planning, STI prevention and testing, pregnancy options and care, healthy relationships, and mental health help-seeking.
3. Tap a module card to select it.
4. Tap the start/continue action to open that module's quiz.
5. Each scenario shows a short story with answer choices.
6. Tap one answer, then move to the next scenario with **Next** / the arrow button.
7. A progress bar shows how far you are through the module.
8. After answering, the correct answer is revealed with a short explanation.
9. Use **Back** / the back arrow to review the previous scenario.
10. When every scenario is answered, a celebration panel shows your score out of the total, with a **Retry** option to run the module again.
11. Use the back button at the top to return to the module catalogue at any time.
12. Modules are available in English, Twi, Ewe and Hausa and follow the app language.

## A14. Myth Buster

1. Select **Myth Buster** in the navigation.
2. Myths are listed with their category labels, 10 per page.
3. Tap a myth card to expand it and reveal the fact that corrects it.
4. Tap the same card again to collapse it.
5. Use the **copy** icon on a fact to copy it to your clipboard — a confirmation toast appears.
6. Use the pagination arrows at the bottom to move between pages; the current page and total are shown.
7. Changing the app language changes the myths shown.

## A15. Support

1. Select **Support** in the navigation.
2. The first card is the **legal notice**: the age of consent in Ghana is 16, and abuse should be reported to the authorities.
3. Below it is a grid of hotline cards. Each card shows the type (for example abuse/violence, mental health, SRH information), the organisation, a short description and a red **Call Now** button with the number.
4. Tap **Call Now** on your phone to dial directly.
5. The dark bar at the bottom repeats the emergency guidance.
6. When in doubt, call **1221** — the free, confidential Lydia Contact Center line.

## A16. Pharmacy Hub (Health Access Hub)

1. Select **Pharmacy Hub** in the navigation.
2. Two tabs are available: **DKT Products** and **Regional Clinics**.

**DKT Products tab**

1. This tab is open by default and shows the DKT Ghana product catalogue.
2. Open a product group to read the product name, description and usage information.
3. Contact details for DKT Ghana (Dzorwulu, Accra · info@dktghana.org) are listed on the page.

**Regional Clinics tab**

1. Tap **Regional Clinics**.
2. A search box sits at the top: *"Search clinic, location, owner, RCR, or phone"*.
3. Type a clinic name, town, owner, RCR number or phone number — the list filters as you type.
4. Region groups appear as cards showing how many facility contacts each contains.
5. Tap a region card to expand it; tap again to collapse it.
6. Each facility entry shows its number, name, location, owner and phone number.

**Finding a place nearby**

1. Use the search/locate buttons on the page to search Google Maps for pharmacies or clinics.
2. Allow location access when asked for the closest results; if you refuse, the search still runs without your position.
3. A map dialog opens with the results; use the external link to continue in Google Maps.

## A17. Settings

Select **Settings** in the navigation. The page is divided into cards.

**Identity & Profile**

1. **Chatbot Name** — change what the assistant calls itself.
2. **Your Nickname** — set or clear your display name (leave empty to stay anonymous).
3. Tap **Save** to apply both names.

**Language**

1. Four buttons: **English**, **Twi**, **Ewe**, **Hausa**.
2. The active language is highlighted in red.
3. Tapping a language switches the whole app immediately (the same choice is also available at the bottom of the sidebar).

**Message Retention**

1. Choose how long chats are kept: **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**.
2. Older messages are dropped from the device when the app loads after the chosen window.

**Data for Good**

1. The **Anonymous analytics** switch controls whether anonymous usage statistics are sent.
2. It is off only if you turn it off; no names, phone numbers or identifiers are included.
3. Changing it is saved immediately.

**Install App**

1. Tap **Re-enable Install Prompt** to make the install banner appear again after you dismissed it.
2. A confirmation toast appears.

**Danger Zone**

1. **Clear Conversation** — deletes the current chat (see [A20](#a20-clearing-your-chat-and-ending-your-session)).
2. **Logout** — ends the session and resets your settings on this device.

## A18. Installing the app on your phone or computer

The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA): it installs without an app store and opens like a normal app.

1. Wait for the install prompt card: **"Install Lydia Contact Center — Add to your home screen for quick access."**
2. Tap **Install** to install, or **Later** to dismiss it.
3. If you dismissed it, re-enable it any time from **Settings → Install App → Re-enable Install Prompt**.
4. **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install** in the prompt, or use the browser menu **⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen**.
5. **iPhone/iPad (Safari):** tap the **Share** icon, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
6. **Desktop (Chrome/Edge):** use the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu **Install Lydia Contact Center**.
7. Once installed, launch it from your home screen or app list. It opens full-screen with no browser bars.
8. Installed copies keep working offline for pages you have already visited.

> The staff dashboard installs separately and only captures the dashboard — see [C10](#c10-installing-the-dashboard-as-an-app).

## A19. Quick exit (panic) screen

Use this if someone approaches and you need the screen to look harmless immediately.

1. Tap the red **PANIC** button in the top header (sitting next to the consultant button).
2. The screen instantly switches to a working grey calculator: number pad, arithmetic keys, **C/AC**, **⌫**, **=**, and a **Back** key.
3. The calculator really calculates, so it looks normal to anyone watching.
4. To return to the app, tap **Back** (the teal key at the top of the pad).
5. The quick exit is recorded as a safety event so the team knows it was used; no one is contacted automatically.

## A20. Clearing your chat and ending your session

**Clear the current conversation**

1. Use **Clear Conversation** in the sidebar, or **Settings → Danger Zone → Clear Conversation**.
2. A confirmation dialog warns that all messages in the conversation will be deleted.
3. Choose **Clear Chat** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep them.
4. When confirmed, the messages are removed from the device, the server-side session is cleared, and a **Chat history cleared** toast appears.

**End your session (log out)**

1. Use **End Session** in the sidebar, or **Settings → Danger Zone → Logout**.
2. A confirmation dialog explains that this resets your settings and clears your data on the device.
3. Choose **End Session** to confirm.
4. Your session data is cleared, onboarding starts again next time, and a confirmation toast appears.

## A21. Offline use

1. If your connection drops, a banner appears at the top of the app.
2. Pages you have already opened keep working from the installed cache.
3. Chat messages cannot reach the server while offline — they fail with the connection message described in [A8](#a8-safe-chat--sending-messages).
4. When the connection returns, the banner disappears and normal use resumes.

## A22. Client troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The chatbot answer says it cannot connect | Check your internet, then resend. Tap the headset for a live counselor, or call 1221. |
| "AI assistant paused" banner | The care team turned the AI off. Use the headset or call 1221; live chat still works. |
| I asked for a counselor and nothing happened | Stay in the app so the queue can reach you, keep the tab open, and check the header button state (queued → live). Call 1221 if urgent. |
| Voice input does nothing | Allow microphone permission, use Chrome/Edge/Safari, or type instead. |
| Audio playback is silent | Check device volume and the media volume; tap the speaker icon again. |
| The install prompt never appears | The browser may not support installation or you dismissed it: **Settings → Install App → Re-enable Install Prompt**. |
| My messages disappeared | Retention is set to a short window, or the chat was cleared. Change retention under Settings. |
| The page shows an old version | Close and reopen the app, or reload the browser tab. |

# Part B — Support consultant manual

## B1. Signing in

1. Open the dashboard address in your browser: **`/dashboard`** (for example `https://your-domain/dashboard`).
2. The **Staff Dashboard Login** page appears with the heading *Secure access for Admin and Support Consultant accounts.*
3. Enter your **work email**.
4. Enter your **account password**.
5. Click **Access Dashboard**. The button shows **Verifying…** while it checks.
6. On success you are taken to the dashboard and a toast confirms **Consultant access granted**.
7. On failure the form shows the reason — invalid credentials, inactive account, or role not permitted.
8. Your session is remembered; reopening the dashboard signs you straight back in until it expires or you sign out.

> Accounts are created by an admin under **Admin Accounts**. There is no self-registration.

## B2. Dashboard tour

**Sidebar (left, always visible from tablet width up)**

- **Overview** — KPIs and charts (admins and supervisors)
- **Support Counselor** — the live takeover queue and your cases (this is your home screen)
- **Conversations** — full chat log and takeover controls
- **Safety & Crisis** — crisis analytics
- Your own name, role and **Sign out** at the bottom

Items you are not permitted to see are hidden for your role.

**Header (top)**

- **☰ Menu** on phones to open the sidebar drawer; section name displayed next to it.
- Amber **"N waiting in queue"** pill when live requests are waiting — tap it to jump to the queue.
- **Search… ⌘K** — the command palette.
- **Bot live / Bot shut down** pill — visible to admins only.
- **Install** button — installs the dashboard as an app.
- **Notifications** bell with a red count badge.
- **Avatar menu** — profile, work status, sign out.

## B3. Work status and profile menu

1. Click your **avatar** at the top-right.
2. The menu shows your name, email, role badge and current status.
3. Under **Work Status**, pick one:
   - **Available** (green)
   - **In Consultation** (blue)
   - **On Break** (amber)
   - **Offline** (grey)
4. A toast confirms the new status, and the coloured dot on your avatar changes.
5. The menu also offers navigation shortcuts and **Sign Out**.

> Set **In Consultation** before a long chat and **On Break** when you step away, so the team can see your availability.

## B4. Notifications and alerts

1. A **chime** plays and a desktop notification fires when a new live request arrives (browser notifications must be allowed).
2. The **bell** in the header shows a red badge with the number of waiting items (capped display of **10+**).
3. Click the bell to open the list of the 10 most recent requests. Urgent items are highlighted in red with an **URGENT** tag.
4. Click any item to jump straight to that conversation.
5. If notifications are blocked, the bell panel shows **Enable Alerts** — click it and allow notifications in the browser prompt.
6. **All caught up!** means there are no pending items.
7. In-app pop-up toasts for new requests are intentionally off; the bell and the chime are the alert channels.

## B5. The Support Counselor queue

1. Select **Support Counselor** in the sidebar (your default screen as a consultant).
2. The top row shows counters: waiting, active, and resolved cases for the period.
3. **Waiting Queue** card (left): each waiting request shows the client's nickname/Anonymous, their age range and the request time, with a red **Takeover Needed** tag.
4. Click **Take Over** on a card to claim the case:
   1. The request is assigned to you.
   2. Its status moves to **active**.
   3. A toast confirms **Took over chat for …**.
   4. The card leaves the waiting queue and appears under your cases.
5. **My Assigned Cases** card (right) lists your assigned and active cases:
   - **Chat** — opens the conversation panel for that client.
   - **Connect** — marks an assigned case as actively connected.
   - **Resolve** — opens the resolution form (see [B9](#b9-resolving-a-case-with-handover-notes)).
6. The queue refreshes automatically every few seconds; you can also refresh manually if a button is provided.
7. Admins and supervisors additionally see a **person filter** on the resolved table to review any counselor's cases.

## B6. Opening a conversation

1. From the queue, click **Chat** on a case. The dashboard switches to **Conversations** and opens that client's detail panel on the right.
2. You can also open any conversation directly: **Conversations** in the sidebar → the **Conversation Log** table → click a row.
3. The panel header shows the client (nickname or Anonymous), the shortened session ID, a platform tag (**WhatsApp** or **Web**), the language, the status badge, the start time and the message count.
4. Status badges mean:
   - **AI Bot Active** — the bot is answering, no human involved.
   - **Queue Bridge (AI)** — the bot is holding the client while they wait for a human.
   - **Takeover Needed** — the client asked for a human, or a safety escalation fired.
   - **Counselor Active** — a counselor is handling the chat.
5. The full message history is shown; messages load in a skeleton view first, then fill in.
6. Closing the panel is done with the **✕** at its top-right, or by clicking the dark backdrop.
7. While the panel is open it refreshes every 3 seconds, so new client messages appear without reloading.

## B7. Taking over a chat and replying

1. With a conversation open, find the **control bar** under the header.
2. If the conversation is escalated you see **Live takeover requested** and a red **Take Over Now** button.
3. If the bot is handling it normally you see **"Bot is responding automatically"** with a **Take Over Chat** button.
4. Click either takeover button. It shows **Taking over…** while it works.
5. When takeover succeeds:
   - The status becomes **Counselor Active**.
   - The bar reads **Live takeover in progress**.
   - The reply box appears at the bottom of the panel.
6. Type your reply in the box *"Send message to … as counselor…"*.
7. Click **Send** (or press Enter). The message is delivered to the client and appended to the thread.
8. The client sees it labelled **Live Agent / Live Counselor**, and their chat banner switches to **Live Consultant Chat**.
9. If another counselor already owns the chat you see a padlock notice: *"Locked — this conversation is currently handled by another counselor."* You cannot send until they release it. This protects the client from crossed replies.
10. If the takeover request fails, the reason is written to the browser console; reload the panel and try again.

> The bot is automatically silenced during a takeover, so the client is talking to you only.

## B8. Releasing a chat back to the bot

1. In the control bar of a taken-over conversation, click **Release to Bot**.
2. The button shows **Releasing…** while it works.
3. On success the status returns to **AI Bot Active**, the reply box is replaced by the hint *"Click Take Over Chat above to message this client directly"*, and the bot resumes answering.
4. Release as soon as your part of the conversation is finished and you have not yet closed the case — or resolve the case instead (next section).

## B9. Resolving a case with handover notes

1. In **My Assigned Cases**, click **Resolve** on an active case (or use the resolve action on a resolved table row's case).
2. The **Resolve & Handover Case** dialog opens.
3. Choose a **Resolution Outcome**:
   - Information & Counseling Provided
   - Referred to PPAG Clinic
   - Emergency / Crisis Hotline Shared
   - Follow-up Required
   - User Inactive / Disconnected
4. Write **Handover / Clinical Notes (Internal)** — a summary for medical audit and supervisor review.
5. Click **Confirm & Close Case** (shows **Saving…**). **Cancel** or **✕** closes without saving.
6. The case moves to the **Resolved Cases & Handover Notes** table at the bottom of the queue page.
7. The table lists: resolved time, client (with age and gender), counselor, conversation link and your notes.
8. Filter the table by date with the date picker, or clear it with the **✕** beside the field.
9. Admins and supervisors can also filter by counselor with the **All Counselors** dropdown.

> Notes are internal. Clients never see them.

## B10. Your shift checklist

1. Sign in and check the **Notifications** bell for anything waiting.
2. Set **Work Status → Available**.
3. Open **Support Counselor** and claim waiting cases with **Take Over**.
4. For each case: read the history, click **Take Over Chat**, reply, and keep the tone calm and non-judgmental.
5. If a client is in danger, share the hotline numbers from [D3](#d3-emergency-and-support-numbers) and escalate immediately.
6. When finished, click **Resolve**, choose the outcome, write handover notes, and confirm.
7. Release any chat you no longer need with **Release to Bot**.
8. Set **Work Status → Offline** and sign out at the end of your shift.

## C9. Bot kill switch (admins only)

Use this when the AI must stop replying immediately — a safety incident, a bad answer wave, or maintenance — while counselors keep talking to clients.

**Turning the bot off**

1. Sign in as an **admin**. The pill is only rendered for admins; supervisors and consultants never see it.
2. In the dashboard header, find the grey pill labelled **Bot live** with a power icon.
3. Click it.
4. Read the confirmation dialog: *"New messages stop reaching the bot API until an admin restarts it. Live counselor chat and the support queue keep working."*
5. Click **Shut down bot** to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.
6. The pill turns red and reads **Bot shut down**, and a warning toast confirms the shutdown.
7. On the client side, visitors immediately see the amber **"AI assistant paused"** banner, and sending produces the paused notice instead of a bot answer.

**What still works while the bot is off**

- Clients can still request a live counselor with the headset button.
- Counselor replies still reach clients, and the takeover/reply flow is unchanged.
- Support queue, conversations, analytics and exports are unaffected.
- No request is made to the bot API at all — the block happens before the network call.

**Turning the bot back on**

1. Click the red **Bot shut down** pill.
2. It switches back to **Bot live** immediately and a success toast confirms the restart.
3. Clients' paused banners disappear and the AI answers again.

**Important limits**

- The switch is stored in the browser where it was set (local storage), and stays in sync across tabs of that browser.
- It does **not** reach other people's devices: a client on another browser or phone is only blocked once the backend itself enforces the flag. If you need a global shutdown, the API must check the same switch server-side.
- The switch records who flipped it and when, and survives page reloads in that browser.
- Clearing browser data resets it to **Bot live**.

## C10. Installing the dashboard as an app

The dashboard installs as its **own** app, separate from the client app, and only opens `/dashboard`.

1. Sign in to the dashboard.
2. Click the **Install** (download icon) button in the header.
3. The browser's install prompt appears.

**If the prompt does not appear (not supported, or already installed):**

1. The app shows a toast with manual instructions:
   - **Chrome/Edge desktop:** install icon in the address bar, or menu → **Install / Apps → Install this site as an app**.
   - **Android Chrome:** menu **⋮ → Add to Home screen**.
   - **iPhone/iPad Safari:** **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Firefox:** does not support installation from the address bar; use Chrome or Edge.
2. Installing requires a secure connection (HTTPS) or localhost.

**After installing**

1. The dashboard opens from its own icon, starts directly at `/dashboard` and stays inside the dashboard when you navigate.
2. The button disappears once the app is installed on that device.
3. Installing from the client app is separate and opens the client experience — see [A18](#a18-installing-the-app-on-your-phone-or-computer).

## C11. Using the dashboard on a phone or tablet

1. From tablet width (768 px) up, the sidebar is docked permanently.

3. The header stays compact: search collapses to an icon-free button, notification text hides, badges stay visible.

## D5. Privacy promises

1. **No account needed for clients** — no email, phone number or name is required.
2. **Anonymous sessions** — the client is identified by a random session ID, plus an optional nickname they choose.
3. **On-device storage** — chat history lives in the browser and is cleared on logout or when retention expires.
4. **Optional analytics** — anonymous usage statistics are only sent while the **Anonymous analytics** switch is on.
5. **Internal notes stay internal** — counselor handover and clinical notes are visible to staff only.
6. **Encrypted, confidential** — the client app shows an *Encrypted* indicator and every screen repeats that the conversation is private.
7. **Quick exit** — the panic button disguises the app as a calculator in one tap.
